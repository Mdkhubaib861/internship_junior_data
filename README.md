# Shopping Trends Analysis – Excel Data Analytics Internship

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing shopping trends and customer purchasing behaviour using Microsoft Excel. It was completed as part of a 5-week Junior Data Analytics Internship with Yuvaintern.

The project covers the complete data analytics workflow, starting from data cleaning and preparation to data analysis, visualization, forecasting, and comprehensive reporting.

The main objective is to identify meaningful patterns in customer purchases, understand product category performance, analyse seasonal trends, and communicate business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard and a final report.

# Weekly Project Breakdown

## Week 1: Data Cleaning and Preparation

**Objective:** Clean and prepare the raw shopping dataset for further analysis.

### Activities Performed

- Imported and examined the raw shopping dataset.
- Reviewed column names and data structure.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Checked for missing values.
- Examined data types and consistency.
- Prepared the dataset for further analysis.

### Dataset Information

The dataset contains approximately 3,900 records and 19 columns.

Important columns include:

- Customer ID
- Age
- Gender
- Item Purchased
- Category
- Purchase Amount (USD)
- Location
- Size
- Color
- Season
- Review Rating
- Subscription Status
- Payment Method
- Shipping Type
- Discount Applied
- Promo Code
- Previous Purchases
- Preferred Payment Method
- Frequency of Purchases

### Outcome

The dataset was prepared for further analysis. The initial cleaning checks reported no duplicate records and no missing values.

---

## Week 2: Data Analysis and KPI Calculation

**Objective:** Analyse the prepared dataset and calculate important business metrics.

### Activities Performed

- Calculated total customer records.
- Calculated total purchase amount.

- Calculated average customer rating.
- Used Excel formulas and functions for analysis.
- Organised key performance indicators.

## Week 4: Forecasting and Trend Analysis

**Objective:** Explore seasonal trends and apply basic forecasting techniques.

### Activities Performed

- Analysed seasonal purchase amounts.
- Calculated moving averages.
- Applied regression analysis.
- Calculated slope and intercept.
- Examined the relationship between seasonal sequence and purchase amount.
- Prepared a forecasting analysis sheet.

### Seasonal Purchase Analysis

| Season | Purchase Amount |
|---|---:|
| Spring | 58,679 |
| Summer | 55,777 |
| Fall | 60,018 |
| Winter | 58,607 |

### Forecasting Results

- Moving Average 1: 58,158
- Moving Average 2: 58,134
- Regression Slope: 402.5
- Regression Intercept: 57,264
- R-squared: Approximately 0.0848
- Exploratory Season 5 Estimate: 59,276.5

### Outcome

The analysis identified seasonal differences and demonstrated basic forecasting techniques. The regression estimate is exploratory and should not be treated as a reliable business forecast because the model uses only four seasonal observations.

---

## Week 5: Comprehensive Reporting and Insight Communication

**Objective:** Combine the findings from the previous four weeks into a comprehensive business report.

### Activities Performed

- Compiled the previous Excel analysis.
- Integrated key performance indicators.
- Explained category-wise purchasing patterns.
- Interpreted seasonal sales performance.
- Included dashboard and Pivot Table screenshots.
- Presented forecasting results.
- Identified business implications.
- Prepared actionable recommendations.
- Created a comprehensive final report.

### Key Business Insights

1. Clothing represents the largest purchase category in the dataset.
2. Accessories are the second-largest category.
3. Fall has the highest seasonal purchase amount.
4. Summer has the lowest seasonal purchase amount.
5. The average customer rating is approximately 3.75 out of 5.
6. The dashboard helps communicate important shopping trends visually.

### Recommendations

- Monitor inventory levels in the Clothing category.
- Explore cross-selling opportunities for Accessories.
- Investigate factors contributing to Fall performance.
- Analyse customer ratings to identify service improvements.
- Evaluate subscription engagement.
- Improve forecasting by collecting more time-based data.
- Maintain consistent currency formatting in reports and dashboards.

### Outcome

A comprehensive report was prepared to communicate the analysis, visualizations, forecasting results, business insights, and recommendations.
